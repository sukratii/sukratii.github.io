I study how AI systems make decisions—and whether those decisions truly align with human values. My research began with modeling how humans navigate collective decisions under uncertainty, but as AI models approach human-level intelligence, I pivoted to the question that now drives my work: how do we evaluate and align these systems before they act autonomously? Today, I develop behavioral evaluations for LLM agents, drawing on cognitive science and social psychology to test whether AI agents are genuinely capable or merely gaming their evaluations.
