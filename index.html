Research

What I Work On

My research sits at the intersection of behavioral science and AI safety. I surface LLM behavioral artifacts using controlled experiments from social psychology, and develop evaluation frameworks for agentic systems deployed in high-stakes environments.

🛡️

Agentic AI Evaluation

Developing evaluation-awareness metrics for autonomous LLM agents. Co-developed novel evaluation frameworks for agentic AI alignment at Amazon Science, now adopted org-wide. Live demo at NeurIPS 2025.

🧠

LLM Behavioral Benchmarking

Designing controlled LLM agent evaluations using established behavioral economics and social psychology experiments. Building a social science benchmark comparing model behavior against human experimental data.

🛡️

Capability vs. Compliance

Measuring whether AI agents are genuinely capable or merely gaming evaluations. Research on evaluation-aware software engineering agents (ICLR 2026, Agents in the Wild workshop).

⚖️

Human Decision Heuristics

Modeling anticipatory human behavior in safety-critical systems under uncertainty. Published in Water Resources Research (2025), applying decision heuristics to understand how humans and AI navigate risk.

📊

Applied ML & NLP

Domain-augmented knowledge graphs with transformer-based entity recognition. LLM fine-tuning (LongLoRA) achieving 88% F1. LSTM forecasting pipelines and RAG-enhanced explanation systems deployed in production.

👥

AI Alignment via Cognitive Science

Applying cognitive science theories to AI alignment. Evaluating whether LLM agents exhibit prosocial or antisocial goals, and whether they fake alignment in social scenarios—a behavioral-level safety layer.
